2Information we collect

  • Telegram account data: your Telegram ID, and, if provided, your first name and username, used only to identify your account.
  • Profile and preference data you enter in the App: language, theme, notification settings, and mentorship topic selections.
  • Content you create: chat messages with mentors/mentees, journal entries, Bible-streak activity, and support tickets.

3How we use your information

  • To operate core features: matching you with a mentor, powering real-time chat, tracking streaks and journaling.
  • To keep the community safe: message moderation, handling reports, and support tickets.
  • To send the notifications you've opted into (new messages, session reminders, daily verse, streak reminders).
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

4Anonymity between members

Mentors and mentees are matched and chat without seeing each other's real Telegram name or username inside the App's peer-support features, unless you choose to share it yourself. We still retain your Telegram ID internally for moderation and safety purposes.

5Who we share data with

  • Telegram — as the messaging platform the Mini App runs on.
  • Supabase — our database provider, which stores App data on our behalf.
  • Jitsi Meet — used to host video counseling sessions.
  • Render — our hosting provider.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6Data retention
