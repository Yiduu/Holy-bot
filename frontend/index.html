Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about Holy Bot.

What is Holy Bot?
Holy Bot is a faith-centered Christian mentorship platform designed to provide anonymous support, biblical recovery, accountability, and 1-on-1 spiritual mentoring.
Is my identity anonymous and safe?
Yes, completely! Your real name, phone number, and Telegram identity are never exposed. You are only identified by your custom nickname or anonymous ID.
How do I connect with a mentor?
Visit the Mentors tab, review mentor bios and focus areas, and tap "Request". When a mentor accepts, you will be connected for 1-on-1 chats and scheduled audio sessions.
How do scheduled sessions work?
Mentors create 1-on-1 or group session slots. You will get a notification reminder before the scheduled time with an instant join button.
How can I contact admin for support?
Tap "Contact Admin" or message our admin directly on Telegram at @YIDIDIYATAMIRUU. You can also submit support requests directly in the app.
